# CivicDesk Vision

> Navigating government rules, public benefits, and official records should not take a lawyer, a navigator, or luck.

## Core Principles

- **Whole-journey, cross-agency structure:** People do not live inside agency org charts. CivicDesk organizes guides around real-world life events and multi-agency sequences (for example, how a name or record mismatch affects Medicaid enrollment).
- **Dated primary sources:** Every rule, deadline, and fee links directly to a dated primary source so readers and advocates can verify what changed and when.
- **Shared-computer privacy:** Designed for public libraries, clinics, and shared household devices with zero cookies, zero analytics, and `Referrer-Policy: no-referrer`.

## Growth Path

CivicDesk launches with two active desks:
- `/medicaid/` — 2027 Medicaid Work and Activity Rules (Oregon, Washington, Utah)
- `/id-records/` — Legal Name, ID, and Vital Records (Oregon, Washington, Utah, and federal agencies)

From this foundation, CivicDesk will expand into `/public-records/` and additional public-interest desks using the same verified, plain-language architecture.
