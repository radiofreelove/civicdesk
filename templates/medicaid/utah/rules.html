A new choice for states

On Nov. 13, 2026, the federal government gave states a choice through June 30, 2027 not to turn anyone down or end coverage solely for not meeting work or activity rules (a hold harmless period). As of Nov. 21, 2026, Utah has not announced a choice, and its pages do not say what Utah will choose. {{ src('MED-S41') }} {{ src('MED-U01') }} {{ src('MED-U02') }}

{% call m.deeper('Who the rules cover, lookback windows & federal mitigation timing', 'ut-rules-scope-detail') %}
  • Who: Adults ages 19 to 64 in Adult Expansion Medicaid or Targeted Adult Medicaid (TAM); other Utah Medicaid programs do not have these rules. {{ src('MED-U01') }}
  • When they start: Jan. 1, 2027 for new applicants (checking the month before you apply); renewals due March 2027 or later for current members (checking any 1 month since your last approval or renewal). Reviews run every 6 months instead of 12. {{ src('MED-U01') }} {{ src('MED-U04') }} {{ src('MED-U13') }}
  • Federal choice rules: States requesting hold harmless from Jan. 1, 2027 were asked to notify CMS by Dec. 11, 2026. Income and other rules still apply. Read Utah's community engagement page. {{ src('MED-S41') }}

Meet the rules any one way

{{ m.icon('check') }}Way 1

1. Income of at least $580 a month

Utah's pages say "earning at least $580 a month" ($7.25 × 80 hours, or about $134 a week; seasonal workers can average over 6 months). The federal rule counts combined Medicaid household income while checking each adult's hours separately—ask DWS how it counts yours. {{ src('MED-U01') }} {{ src('MED-S27') }}

{{ m.icon('school-cap') }}Way 2

2. School at least half-time

You attend school on a schedule your school calls half-time or more. {{ src('MED-U01') }}

{{ m.icon('hours-meter') }}Way 3

3. 80 hours a month (~20 hours a week)

Combine work, job training, school, or unpaid volunteering with an organized community program. Not sure if an activity counts? Ask DWS or a free helper. {{ src('MED-U01') }}

Proof & review rules in Utah

  • Records checked first: DWS checks work, school, and benefit databases before asking you for anything.
  • Utah law requires proof: Under Utah Code 26B-3-142.1 (effective Jan. 1, 2027), Utah must use records or documents and cannot rely only on your own word. {{ src('MED-U39') }}
  • 30-day form deadline & 6-month reviews: If DWS asks for a form, you may need to return it within 30 days, and proof may be checked at 6-month renewals. See every exemption or take the 1-minute check. {{ src('MED-U04') }}
