American Indian and Alaska Native members

Utah's letter asks members to send proof of status by Jan. 1, 2027. Once verified, your renewal stays once every 12 months; if unverified, you may have to meet the work rules. Ways to send papers to DWS

{% call m.deeper('Accepted Tribal proof documents (send 1 copy per member)', 'ut-tribal-proof-list') %}
  • Tribal ID or enrollment card (or enrollment number)
  • Certificate of Degree of Indian Blood (CDIB) signed by the Bureau of Indian Affairs
  • Indian Health Service (IHS) face sheet with your name and date of birth
  • Tribal court paper with your name and date of birth
  • Any of these for a parent or grandparent, plus a birth certificate or court paper showing how you are related
{% endcall %}

How to tell the state

  1. Electronic check first: If DWS confirms your reason in its records, you do nothing.
  2. Report in myCase or on form 742: Report on your application/renewal, in myCase, or on form 742 (Community Engagement Declaration—ask DWS at 1-866-435-7414).
  3. Include proof: Under Utah Code 26B-3-142.1 (effective Jan. 1, 2027), Utah cannot rely only on your own word and requires proof unless electronic records confirm it. {{ src('MED-U39') }}
