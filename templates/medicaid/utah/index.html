{{ m.icon('alert') }}Deadline

Have medical bills? Read this before Jan. 1, 2027.

Through Dec. 31, 2026, most Utah Medicaid programs can cover care from up to 3 months before the month you apply. {{ src('MED-U02') }}

Starting Jan. 1, 2027, Adult Expansion Medicaid covers care from 1 month before the month you apply. More about past medical bills {{ src('MED-U02') }}

Meet the rules any one way

Utah calls this the community engagement requirement. DWS checks records it already has first for the month before you apply. {{ src('MED-U01') }}

  • {{ m.icon('income') }}Income Utah's pages say earning at least $580 a month (federal rule counts combined household income). {{ src('MED-U01') }} {{ src('MED-S27') }}

  • {{ m.icon('school') }}School You go to school at least half-time. {{ src('MED-U01') }}

  • {{ m.icon('clock') }}80 hours You spend 80 hours a month on work, job training, volunteering, or school. Add them up. {{ src('MED-U01') }}

You may not have to if you

  • Are pregnant, or get Medicaid for the months after a birth {{ src('MED-U05') }}

  • Have a disability, or a health condition that makes daily tasks hard {{ src('MED-U05') }}
  • Are American Indian or Alaska Native, or are/were in foster care
