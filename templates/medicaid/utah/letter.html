Ways to send papers

  • Online: Upload in myCase at jobs.utah.gov/mycase (and keep your address, phone, and email updated there).
  • Mail: Department of Workforce Services, Imaging Operations, PO Box 143245, Salt Lake City, UT 84114-3245.
  • Fax or in person: Fax 1-877-313-4717 or 801-526-9500, or visit any DWS employment center (Mon–Fri, 8 a.m.–5 p.m.). Always use a different address or fax if printed on your letter.
