How to apply for Utah Medicaid

The Department of Workforce Services (DWS) decides who qualifies. Pick any of the 3 ways below—and get free help from Take Care Utah or in your language.

{{ m.icon('online') }}Apply online

Start at jobs.utah.gov/mycase. You do not need a paper application when you apply in myCase, and keeping your address, phone, and email current there helps you avoid missed notices.

{{ m.icon('paper') }}Apply on paper

Mail form 61MED (English PDF) or form 61MED (Spanish PDF) to Department of Workforce Services, PO Box 143245, Salt Lake City, UT 84114-3245, or fax the number printed on your form. Note: the April 1, 2026 paper form does not yet ask about the 2027 work rules, so DWS may ask after you apply.

{{ m.icon('inperson') }}Apply in person & free language help

Find a DWS employment center, or ask DWS for free interpreter help when you call (1-866-435-7414) or visit. More free help and phone numbers
