Utah Medicaid

How to apply for Utah Medicaid

The Department of Workforce Services (DWS) decides who qualifies. Pick any of the 3 ways below—and get free help from Take Care Utah or in your language.

{{ m.onthispage([ ('apply-online', 'Online (myCase)'), ('apply-paper', 'Paper (61MED)'), ('apply-inperson', 'In person'), ('after-apply', 'After you apply') ]) }}

Helpful to have nearby (not required just to start): ID or birth certificate if requested (10-day return window), prior-month work/school/volunteer hours, and proof for any exclusion or exception.

Apply online (myCase)

Start at jobs.utah.gov/mycase and upload proof from your phone or computer. You do not need a paper application when applying in myCase; keep your address, phone, and email current there so you do not miss notices.

Apply on paper (form 61MED)

Mail form 61MED (English PDF) or form 61MED (Spanish PDF) to Department of Workforce Services, PO Box 143245, Salt Lake City, UT 84114-3245, fax the number printed on your form, or call 1-866-435-7414 to request a copy by mail. Because the April 1, 2026 paper form does not yet ask about the 2027 work rules, DWS may follow up after you apply.

Apply in person & free language help

Visit any DWS employment center (Mon–Fri, 8 a.m.–5 p.m.), get free help from Take Care Utah, or ask DWS for free interpreter help when you call (1-866-435-7414) or visit. More free help and phone numbers
