Utah Medicaid

Turned down for Utah Medicaid? You have options.

If DWS turns you down or ends your coverage, you can keep benefits during an appeal, request a phone fair hearing, get free legal help, or reapply with no waiting period.

{{ m.icon('check') }}How to request a DWS fair hearing & free help

How to ask (do not submit via myCase)

  • Form 490: Hearing request (English PDF) or Spanish PDF. Hearings are by phone with a free interpreter if needed; note that continued benefits may have to be repaid if you lose. {{ src('MED-U19') }}
  • Send to DWS Fair Hearings: Mail PO Box 143245, Salt Lake City, UT 84114-3245; fax 877-824-6534; phone 877-837-3247; email fairhearings@utah.gov; or drop off at any DWS employment center.
  • Free help: Utah Legal Services (801-328-8891 or 800-662-4245) and Take Care Utah (801-433-2299). Or retake the 1-minute check and reapply.
