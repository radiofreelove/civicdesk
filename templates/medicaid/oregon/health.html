This is not the full list—many conditions can count, and you can tell OHP even if yours is not listed: {{ src('MED-S03') }}

  • Recovering from major surgery or a serious injury; cancer needing chemotherapy/radiation, or dialysis.
  • Crohn's disease, COPD, diabetes with complications, or cystic fibrosis.
  • Major depression needing ongoing treatment, or a substance use disorder.
  • Down syndrome, cerebral palsy, blindness, or another physical, intellectual, or developmental disability.
