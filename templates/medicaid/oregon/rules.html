Oregon's hold harmless delay (through June 30, 2027)

Governor Tina Kotek directed OHA to use the federal hold harmless option through June 30, 2027 (subject to federal approval). You will not be turned down or lose OHP coverage through June 30, 2027 solely for not meeting work or activity rules—though verification checks still begin Jan. 1, 2027 and standard income and residency rules still apply. {{ src('MED-S01') }} {{ src('MED-S07') }} {{ src('MED-S53') }} {{ src('MED-S41') }}

{% call m.deeper('Proposed Oregon rules (OAR 410-200-0221) & federal lawsuit', 'or-rules-legal-detail') %}

On Nov. 13, 2026, CMS let states request a hold harmless period in their mitigation plans (due Dec. 11, 2026 for a Jan. 1 start). {{ src('MED-S41') }}

Proposed state rules: On Oct. 28, 2026, OHA proposed rules (OAR 410-200-0221; comment closed Nov. 21, 2026) covering new applications from Jan. 1, 2027 and renewals due March 31, 2027 or later. {{ src('MED-S47') }}

Court case: On Nov. 5, 2026, Oregon, Washington, and two other states sued in State of Oregon v. Oz (No. 6:26-cv-02435; oral argument Jan. 12, 2027). No court order has halted the rules. {{ src('MED-S44') }} {{ src('MED-S45') }} {{ src('MED-S46') }}

Meet the rules any one way

{{ m.icon('check') }}Way 1

1. Household income of $580 or more a month

Counts the combined income of your Medicaid household ($7.25 federal minimum wage × 80 hours, or about $134 a week). Two adults in the household do not each need $580. {{ src('MED-S27') }}

{{ m.icon('school-cap') }}Way 2

2. School at least half-time

Your school decides what counts as half-time—including college, career or trade school, high school, and GED programs. {{ src('MED-S01') }}

{{ m.icon('hours-meter') }}Way 3

3. 80 hours a month (~20 hours a week)

Combine paid work, self-employment, work for housing/goods, internships, school, job training, or tracked nonprofit/public volunteering. {{ src('MED-S01') }}

Household & exemption rules

{{ m.analogy(page, 'household-income-vs-individual-hours') }}
  • Hours are checked per adult; income is combined: One person's hours or school do not cover another adult, while the $580 monthly income threshold counts your Medicaid household together. {{ src('MED-S05') }} {{ src('MED-S27') }}
  • Exemptions: People with a disability, pregnancy, caregiving duties, or Medicare do not have to meet these rules. See every exemption or take the 1-minute check. {{ src('MED-S02') }} {{ src('MED-S03') }} {{ src('MED-S04') }}
