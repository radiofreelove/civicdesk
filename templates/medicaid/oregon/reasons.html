How to tell OHP

  1. When you apply, answer every question about work, school, volunteering, and exemptions.
  2. Already applied? Report your exemption in your ONE account at benefits.oregon.gov, call 800-699-9075, or visit an ODHS office. {{ src('MED-S08') }}
