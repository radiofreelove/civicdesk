  • Excluded groups: People 18 or younger, 65 or older, getting OHP for disability or pregnancy, or enrolled in Medicare (show your Medicare card if asked). {{ src('MED-S02') }} {{ src('MED-S03') }}

  • Caregiving & recovery: Caring for a household/family member has no hour minimum; caring for a non-relative outside your home requires 80 hours/month.   • Hospital & travel hardships: At least 1 inpatient night in a hospital, nursing, or behavioral health facility, or 7+ days away from home in a month for medical care for you, your child, or a tax dependent. {{ src('MED-S03') }}

How to tell OHP

  1. When you apply, answer every question about work, school, volunteering, and exemptions.
  2. Already applied? Report your exemption in your ONE account at benefits.oregon.gov, call 800-699-9075, or visit an ODHS office. {{ src('MED-S08') }}
