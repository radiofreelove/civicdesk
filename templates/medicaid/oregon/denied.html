Oregon Health Plan (OHP)

Turned down? You have options.

This page explains what to do if OHP says no or your coverage ends.

The short version

  • Apply again: You can apply again any time.
  • Ask for a hearing: Your letter says how, and by when.
  • Keep benefits: You may be able to keep your benefits during a hearing. Ask.
  • Free legal help: Call the Public Benefits Hotline.
  • Next: How to apply, or free help and phone numbers.

A new choice for states

Governor Tina Kotek directed OHA to use the federal hold harmless choice through June 30, 2027 (subject to federal approval). OHA says you will not be turned down for OHP or lose coverage through June 30, 2027 if you do not meet the work or activity rules. {{ src('MED-S07') }} {{ src('MED-S53') }} {{ src('MED-S41') }}

This follows a Nov. 13, 2026 federal choice. Through June 30, 2027, a state may choose not to turn anyone down, and not to end anyone's coverage, only for not meeting the work or activity rules. A state can still say no, or end coverage, for other reasons, such as income. More about this choice {{ src('MED-S41') }}

Court case: On Nov. 5, 2026, Oregon, Washington, and two other states sued the federal government over the work or activity rules (State of Oregon v. Oz, No. 6:26-cv-02435). Oral argument is set for Jan. 12, 2027. As of Nov. 21, 2026, no court order has changed these rules. {{ src('MED-S44') }} {{ src('MED-S45') }} {{ src('MED-S46') }}

Apply again

You can apply again any time. OHP looks at the month before your new application. {{ src('MED-S06') }} {{ src('MED-S04') }}

If you meet the rules now, or a reason fits you now, you may qualify. Take the 1-minute check.

Ask for a hearing

A hearing is a way to challenge OHP's decision. Your letter says how to ask for one and the deadline. {{ src('MED-S04') }} {{ src('MED-S14') }}

When you ask, you can also ask to keep your benefits while you wait for the decision. {{ src('MED-S04') }}

Free legal help

{{ m.icon('check') }}Good to know

Public Benefits Hotline

Run by Legal Aid Services of Oregon and the Oregon Law Center.

Call 503-241-4111 or 800-520-5292.

Income limits apply. Hours change, so check their hours before you call. People in Jackson County are sent to a different office. {{ src('MED-S25') }}

Disability question? Ask Disability Rights Oregon for help. {{ src('MED-S26') }}

Other coverage

You can look at private health plans at OregonHealthCare.gov or call 855-268-3767. {{ src('MED-S04') }} {{ src('MED-S20') }}

Heads up

If OHP turned you down because of the work or activity rules, you may not get help paying for a Marketplace plan. {{ src('MED-S04') }} {{ src('MED-S06') }}

Care while you sort it out

  • Community health centers charge what you can afford. Find a community health center. {{ src('MED-S04') }} {{ src('MED-S21') }}
  • Nonprofit hospitals have programs to help pay bills. Ask about financial help. {{ src('MED-S04') }}
