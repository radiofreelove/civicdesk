Nov. 2026 update: what Oregon's enforcement delay covers

Governor Tina Kotek directed OHA to use the federal hold harmless option through June 30, 2027 (subject to federal approval). Standard eligibility rules such as income still apply, and OHP still checks work-rule or exemption status starting Jan. 1, 2027. {{ m.icon('alert') }}Unpaid medical bills

Have recent medical bills? Apply before Jan. 1, 2027 if you can.

Applying by Dec. 31, 2026 keeps the full 3-month retroactive bill window. Starting Jan. 1, 2027, most adults ages 19 to 64 can get help with unpaid bills from only 1 month before applying (2 months for children, seniors, pregnancy, and disability). Past medical bills details {{ src('MED-S01') }}

Meet the rules any one way

OHP checks its own payroll and school databases first for the month before you apply. {{ src('MED-S01') }} {{ src('MED-S04') }}

You may not have to if you

  • Are pregnant, or were in the last 12 months {{ src('MED-S03') }}
  • Care for a child 13 or younger, or for someone with a disability {{ src('MED-S03') }}
  • Have a serious or disabling health condition {{ src('MED-S03') }}
  • Are American Indian or Alaska Native, or were in foster care at 18 and are under 26 {{ src('MED-S02') }}

What to do

  1. Take the 1-minute check. Your answers stay on your device.
  2. Apply online, by phone, in person, or on paper. If OHP says no, you can apply again any time. {{ src('MED-S06') }}
  3. Answer every question about work, school, and volunteering. If a reason fits you, tell OHP. {{ src('MED-S05') }} {{ src('MED-S08') }}
