Oregon Health Plan (OHP)

How to apply for OHP

There are 4 ways to apply. Pick the one that is easiest for you.

The short version

  • {{ m.icon('online') }}Online: benefits.oregon.gov (no phone call needed)
  • {{ m.icon('paper') }}On paper: the OHP application, form 7210, in 17 languages.
  • {{ m.icon('inperson') }}In person: at an ODHS office, or with a free community partner.
  • {{ m.icon('phone') }}By phone: 800-699-9075 (TTY 711), Monday to Friday, 7 a.m. to 6 p.m.
  • {{ m.icon('helper') }}Help is free: Community partners do not charge.

{{ m.icon('online') }}Apply online

Go to benefits.oregon.gov. Make a ONE account. Then apply. {{ src('MED-S11') }}

Some state pages say ONE.Oregon.gov instead. That is the same ONE system.

Trouble with the website? Call ONE technical support at 833-978-1073.

{{ m.icon('phone') }}Apply by phone

Call ONE Customer Service at 800-699-9075. TTY users can dial 711. Lines are open Monday to Friday, 7 a.m. to 6 p.m. Pacific time. {{ src('MED-S09') }}

In Spanish, call 800-743-9182. In Russian, call 833-687-1508. See all language lines PDF {{ src('MED-S17') }}

{{ m.icon('inperson') }}Apply in person

  • Find an ODHS office near you
  • Find a free community partner near you {{ src('MED-S09') }} {{ src('MED-S16') }}

{{ m.icon('paper') }}Apply on paper

Download the OHP application (form 7210) PDF. It comes in 17 languages. Get it in another language. {{ src('MED-S09') }} {{ src('MED-S10') }}

{{ m.icon('alert') }}Heads up

Heads up about the paper form

The paper form is from May 2020. It does not ask about the new rules. OHP may send you a letter asking about them. {{ src('MED-S10') }} {{ src('MED-S04') }}

Questions about where to send it? Call 800-699-9075.

What to have ready

  • Facts about each person in your household.
  • Income for each person: jobs, self-employment, and other money coming in.
  • Any other health insurance you have.
  • For the new rules: your work, school, or volunteer hours for the month before you apply, or the reason you don't have to meet the rules.

What happens after you apply

  1. {{ m.icon('check') }}Records checked first OHP checks its own records first, like payroll and school records. {{ src('MED-S04') }}
  2. {{ m.icon('letter') }}Decision letter If OHP can confirm everything, you get a letter with its decision. It is called a Notice of Eligibility. {{ src('MED-S04') }}
  3. {{ m.icon('calendar') }}35 days if more is needed If OHP needs more, you get a letter called a Request for Information. You have 35 days to reply. How to answer a letter {{ src('MED-S04') }}

Over the income limit?

  • Check the OHP income limits chart PDF. {{ src('MED-S18') }}
  • OHP Bridge covers adults with income just above the OHP limit. Members pay nothing. {{ src('MED-S13') }}
  • You can also look at private plans at OregonHealthCare.gov or call 855-268-3767. {{ src('MED-S04') }} {{ src('MED-S20') }}
