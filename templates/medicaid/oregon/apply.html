How to apply for OHP

There are 4 ways to apply, and help from certified community partners is always free.

Choose how to apply

Helpful to have nearby (not required just to start): household member names, monthly jobs or income, any other health insurance, and last month's hours or exemption.

  • Online (ONE): Create or sign in to a ONE account at benefits.oregon.gov (tech support: 833-978-1073). {{ src('MED-S11') }}
  • By phone: Call 800-699-9075 (TTY 711), Mon–Fri 7 a.m.–6 p.m. PT. Spanish: 800-743-9182; Russian: 833-687-1508; all language lines PDF. {{ src('MED-S09') }} {{ src('MED-S17') }}
  • In person (free help): Find an ODHS office or find a free certified community partner. {{ src('MED-S09') }} {{ src('MED-S15') }} {{ src('MED-S16') }}
  • On paper (form 7210): Download OHP form 7210 PDF or pick another language (17 languages). Because the May 2020 paper form does not ask about 2027 rules, OHP may mail a follow-up letter. {{ src('MED-S04') }} {{ src('MED-S10') }}
