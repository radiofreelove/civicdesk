Online, phone, in-person & paper details

  • Online: Make a ONE account at benefits.oregon.gov (also called ONE.Oregon.gov). {{ src('MED-S11') }}
  • By phone: 800-699-9075 (TTY 711). Spanish: 800-743-9182; Russian: 833-687-1508; all language lines PDF. {{ src('MED-S09') }} {{ src('MED-S17') }}
  • In person: Find an ODHS office or find a free community partner. {{ src('MED-S15') }} {{ src('MED-S16') }}
  • On paper: Download OHP form 7210 PDF or pick another language. Because the May 2020 paper form does not ask about 2027 rules, OHP may mail a follow-up letter. {{ src('MED-S04') }} {{ src('MED-S10') }}
