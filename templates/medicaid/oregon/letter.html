Oregon Health Plan (OHP)

OHP sent me a letter. Now what?

This page explains the letters and how to answer them on time.

The short version

  • First: Find the due date on the letter.
  • December 2026 notices: OHP plans to send notices to members in December 2026 explaining what to expect for 2027. {{ src('MED-S42') }}
  • Proof needed? A Request for Information means OHP needs more from you. You have 35 days to reply. {{ src('MED-S04') }}
  • Reminder: If you signed up for email, you get a reminder 5 days before it is due. {{ src('MED-S04') }}
  • How to answer: Online, by phone, in person, by fax, or by mail. {{ src('MED-S08') }}
  • Missed the due date? See your options.

Letters you may get

December 2026 member notice
An early notice OHP plans to send in December 2026. It explains how the 2027 work or activity rules may apply to you. {{ src('MED-S42') }}
Notice of Eligibility
OHP's decision about your coverage. {{ src('MED-S04') }}
Request for Information
OHP needs proof or more facts. The due date is on the letter. You have 35 days. {{ src('MED-S04') }}
Notice of Status
Your status for the new rules changed. Your benefits did not change. It helps you get ready for your next renewal. {{ src('MED-S04') }}

Ways to answer

  • Online: your ONE account at benefits.oregon.gov
  • Phone: 800-699-9075 (TTY 711)
  • In person: any local ODHS office
  • Fax: 503-378-5628
  • Mail: PO Box 14015, Salem, OR 97309. Check your letter for the exact address. {{ src('MED-S08') }}
  • With help: a free community partner {{ src('MED-S16') }}

Tips

  • Keep copies: Keep a copy of everything you send, and write down the date you sent it.
  • Case number: Write the case number from your letter on every page you send.
  • Do not wait: Answer as soon as you can. Mail takes time.

{{ m.icon('info') }}Key point

Missed the due date?

If you do not send proof by the due date, OHP can turn you down. You can apply again any time. See your options {{ src('MED-S04') }} {{ src('MED-S06') }}
