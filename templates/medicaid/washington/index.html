{{ m.icon('info') }}Policy update

Nov. 17, 2026 update: what Washington's enforcement delay covers

The Health Care Authority (HCA) announced Washington will pursue delayed enforcement through June 30, 2027 (pending federal approval). Income rules, 6-month renewals (12 months for Tribal members), and verification checks still start Jan. 1, 2027—keep your contact info current in Washington Healthplanfinder. {{ src('MED-WA01') }} {{ src('MED-WA16') }} {{ src('MED-WA22') }} {{ src('MED-WA27') }} {{ src('MED-S41') }}

{{ m.icon('alert') }}Unpaid medical bills

Have recent medical bills? Apply before Jan. 1, 2027 if you can.

Applying before Jan. 1, 2027 keeps the full 3-month retroactive bill window. Starting Jan. 1, 2027, Apple Health for Adults covers unpaid bills from only 1 month before applying (up to 2 months for other programs). Past medical bills details {{ src('MED-WA01') }} {{ src('MED-WA03') }}
