Update, Nov. 17, 2026: Washington delays work-rule enforcement

Washington's announcement: On Nov. 17, 2026, the Health Care Authority (HCA) announced Washington will pursue delayed enforcement of the work rules (pending federal approval). Through June 30, 2027, people in Apple Health for Adults will not lose or be denied coverage solely for not meeting the work rules—though 6-month renewals (Tribal members keep 12-month renewals), verification checks, and the 1-month past-bills limit still start Jan. 1, 2027. {{ m.icon('alert') }}Deadline

Have medical bills? Read this before Jan. 1, 2027.

Before Jan. 1, 2027, Apple Health may pay bills from up to 3 months before the month you apply. {{ src('MED-WA01') }} {{ src('MED-WA03') }}

On Jan. 1, 2027, that window drops to 1 month for Apple Health for Adults. More about past medical bills {{ src('MED-WA01') }}

What to do

  1. Take the 1-minute check. Your answers stay on your device.
  2. Apply online, by phone, on paper, or in person (open all year). {{ src('MED-WA06') }}
  3. Self-attest your income, hours, or exemption; if state data cannot confirm eligibility, reply within 15 days if HCA sends a letter. Only Washington decides. {{ src('MED-WA04') }} {{ src('MED-WA22') }}
