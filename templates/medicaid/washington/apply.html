Choose how to apply

  • Online (Healthplanfinder): Adults under 65 without Medicare apply, renew, or report changes at wahealthplanfinder.org (HCA apply guide). {{ src('MED-WA06') }}
  • By phone: Call Healthplanfinder Support at 1-855-923-4633 (TTY 1-855-627-9604) with free interpreter support. {{ src('MED-WA10') }}
  • On paper (form 18-001P): Mail or fax form 18-001P (fax 1-855-867-4467). {{ src('MED-WA11') }} {{ src('MED-WA12') }}
  • In person (free help): Certified navigators and Apple Health Community Connectors help free of charge (find local help). {{ src('MED-WA06') }}
