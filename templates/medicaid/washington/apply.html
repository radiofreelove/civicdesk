Age 65+, blind, disabled, or on Medicare?

Apply for Apple Health Classic through DSHS at Washington Connection online or call 1-877-501-2233 instead of Healthplanfinder. {{ src('MED-WA06') }} {{ src('MED-WA07') }}

Channel details & reporting changes

  • Online: Apply, renew, or report income/household changes at wahealthplanfinder.org. {{ src('MED-WA06') }}
  • Phone & language help: 1-855-923-4633 (TTY 1-855-627-9604) with free interpreter support. {{ src('MED-WA10') }}
  • Paper: Form 18-001P by mail or fax (1-855-867-4467). {{ src('MED-WA11') }} {{ src('MED-WA12') }}
  • In person: Certified navigators and Apple Health Community Connectors help free of charge. {{ src('MED-WA06') }}
