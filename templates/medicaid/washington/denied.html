Steps to challenge a decision

  • HCA eligibility decisions: File online or call the Office of Administrative Hearings at 1-800-583-8271 (HCA appeal guide). {{ src('MED-WA19') }} {{ src('MED-WA29') }}
  • Managed care health plan decisions: Complete your health plan's grievance/appeal first, then request a hearing within 120 calendar days of the plan's resolution notice. {{ src('MED-WA29') }}
  • Free local helpers: Navigators and Apple Health Community Connectors help free of charge (phone numbers & local help or 1-minute check). {{ src('MED-WA06') }} {{ src('MED-WA08') }} {{ src('MED-WA10') }}
