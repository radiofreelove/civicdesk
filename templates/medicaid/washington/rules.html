Washington Apple Health

The new Apple Health rules, in plain words

This page explains who the rules are for and the 6 ways to meet them.

Not sure if the rules fit you? Take the 1-minute check.

The short version

  • Starts: Jan. 1, 2027 (with work-rule coverage loss delayed through June 30, 2027). The Health Care Authority (HCA) tells members to keep their contact information up to date and read notices or text messages from HCA. {{ src('MED-WA01') }} {{ src('MED-WA27') }}
  • Who: Only people in Apple Health for Adults. {{ src('MED-WA15') }}
  • When the state looks: The month before you apply. At a renewal, one of the 6 months before. {{ src('MED-WA05') }}
  • Records first: Washington checks its own data first.
  • Names: HCA calls these rules work requirements. The federal name is community engagement.

A new choice for states

On Nov. 17, 2026, HCA announced Washington will pursue delayed enforcement of the work rules (pending federal approval). Through June 30, 2027, people in Apple Health for Adults will not lose or be denied coverage for not meeting the work rules. Six-month renewals and the 1-month past-bills limit still start Jan. 1, 2027. {{ src('MED-WA27') }} {{ src('MED-WA22') }}

On Nov. 13, 2026, the federal government gave each state a choice. Through June 30, 2027, a state may choose not to turn anyone down, and not to end anyone's coverage, only for not meeting the work rules. The state must still check whether each person meets the rules or does not have to. The federal name for this is a hold harmless period. {{ src('MED-S41') }}

The rules and checking still start Jan. 1, 2027, and HCA says clients will still be asked to verify. Through June 30, 2027, HCA says no one will lose or be denied Apple Health for not meeting them, pending federal approval of Washington's plan. The choice does not protect anyone from being turned down or losing coverage for other reasons, such as income. {{ src('MED-S41') }} {{ src('MED-WA27') }}

A state has to ask the federal Medicaid agency in its mitigation plan and get its approval. The choice covers applications the state gets, and renewals the state starts, through June 30, 2027. A state may pick a shorter time. A state that wants it from Jan. 1, 2027 was asked to tell the agency by Dec. 11, 2026. {{ src('MED-S41') }}

Court case: On Nov. 5, 2026, Washington, Oregon, and two other states sued the federal government over the work rules (State of Oregon v. Oz, No. 6:26-cv-02435). Oral argument is set for Jan. 12, 2027. As of Nov. 21, 2026, no court order has changed these rules. {{ src('MED-WA14') }} {{ src('MED-S44') }} {{ src('MED-S45') }} {{ src('MED-S46') }}

Who the rules are for

The work rules apply only to Apple Health for Adults. That group is people who:

  • Are age 19 to 64
  • Do not have Medicare
  • Live in Washington
  • Have income under 138 percent of the federal poverty level

Meet the rules any one way

HCA's work requirements page lists 6 ways. You need only one.

{{ m.icon('check') }}Good to know

1. Income

Your household has at least $580 in income a month. {{ src('MED-WA22') }}

Where $580 comes from: 80 hours times the federal minimum wage of $7.25.

Work only part of the year? Seasonal workers may use an average of $580 a month over 6 months.

This is your Medicaid household's monthly MAGI income (Modified Adjusted Gross Income), the same income HCA counts for Apple Health (generally you, your spouse, and people on your tax return; children's income often does not count). It does not have to come from a job.

{{ m.icon('check') }}Good to know

2. Work

You work at least 80 hours a month. {{ src('MED-WA22') }}

Paid work, self-employment, in-kind work, and unpaid work can count. In-kind work means work paid in goods, housing, or services, such as lower rent.

Not sure how your job counts? Ask HCA or a free helper.

{{ m.icon('school-cap') }}Good to know

3. School

You are in an educational program at least half-time. {{ src('MED-WA05') }}

Under the federal rule, this includes high school and GED programs.

Taking fewer credits? You can mix school credits with other hours in way 6 below.

{{ m.icon('check') }}Good to know

4. A work program

You are in a work program at least 80 hours a month. {{ src('MED-WA22') }}

{{ m.icon('check') }}Good to know

5. Community service

You do community service at least 80 hours a month. {{ src('MED-WA22') }}

{{ m.icon('hours-meter') }}Good to know

6. A mix

You do a mix of activities. The total is at least 80 hours a month. {{ src('MED-WA22') }}

You may add up hours of work, community service, a work program, and school.

If you take 1 to 6 school credits, HCA converts your credits into monthly hours so you can add them to other activities: {{ src('MED-WA22') }}

How the state checks

  • Records first: Washington checks its own data first. HCA estimated that it could check about 64 percent of people in Apple Health for Adults this way, or find they are exempt.
  • Your word: You may state your own income, work, or exemption status. HCA calls this self-attestation (including for medical frailty through 2027). If state records cannot confirm eligibility, a letter may ask for proof within 15 days. {{ src('MED-WA04') }} {{ src('MED-WA22') }}
  • Shorter coverage: Starting Jan. 1, 2027, Apple Health for Adults moves from 12-month to 6-month renewals (American Indian and Alaska Native adults keep 12-month renewals). {{ src('MED-WA16') }}
