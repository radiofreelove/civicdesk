Washington's hold harmless delay (through June 30, 2027)

On Nov. 17, 2026, HCA announced Washington will pursue delayed enforcement of the work rules (pending federal approval). Through June 30, 2027, people in Apple Health for Adults will not lose or be denied coverage solely for not meeting the work rules—though 6-month renewals, checking, and the 1-month past-bills limit still start Jan. 1, 2027. {{ src('MED-WA01') }} {{ src('MED-WA03') }} {{ src('MED-WA15') }} {{ src('MED-WA16') }} {{ src('MED-WA22') }} {{ src('MED-WA27') }} {{ src('MED-S41') }}
