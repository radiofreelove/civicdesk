A new choice for states (hold harmless through June 30, 2027)

On Nov. 17, 2026, HCA announced Washington will pursue delayed enforcement of the work rules (pending federal approval). Through June 30, 2027, people in Apple Health for Adults will not lose or be denied coverage solely for not meeting the work rules—though 6-month renewals, checking, and the 1-month past-bills limit still start Jan. 1, 2027. {{ src('MED-WA01') }} {{ src('MED-WA03') }} {{ src('MED-WA15') }} {{ src('MED-WA16') }} {{ src('MED-WA22') }} {{ src('MED-WA27') }} {{ src('MED-S41') }}

How the state checks

  • Data check first: Washington checks its own records first (HCA estimated ~64% of Apple Health for Adults can be confirmed or found exempt automatically).
  • Self-attestation & 15-day proof window: You may state your own income, hours, or exemption (including medical frailty through 2027); if records cannot confirm it, HCA may send a letter giving 15 days to verify. See every exemption or get free help. {{ src('MED-WA04') }} {{ src('MED-WA22') }}
