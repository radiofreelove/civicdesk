What CivicDesk does—and does not do

  • Plain-language translation: Translates statutes, administrative rules, court packets, and agency guidance into direct everyday steps, linking every fee, deadline, and rule to its dated primary source.
  • Not a government office or legal advice: CivicDesk is not run by any court or government agency and does not give legal advice. Only the court or agency decides your case—always confirm current forms and fees on the linked official page.
  • Private by design: Interactive checks run in your browser's local page memory only with zero cookies, zero analytics, and no accounts.

Active service desks

  • 2027 Medicaid Work & Activity Rules (/medicaid/): Plain-language guides and 1-minute checks for the Oregon Health Plan (OHP), Washington Apple Health, and Utah Medicaid.
  • Legal Name, ID & Vital Records (/id-records/): Step-by-step agency sequences for marriage, divorce, court name changes, and sex or gender designation updates across Oregon, Washington, Utah, Social Security, and U.S. passports.

How we verify sources

We prioritize primary government records—published statutes, administrative rules, court orders, official forms, and agency manuals. Automated Policy Wire sweeps flag regulatory or court-docket changes twice a week, and a human maintainer reviews and verifies every update before publication.

Every cited source carries one of four explicit status labels: Official, Not official, Not confirmed, or Our reading. Read what each label means or inspect our registries on GitHub.

Report a mistake

Spot an outdated rule, wrong fee, broken link, or unclear sentence? You can reach us in two ways:

  • By email (no GitHub account needed): Email {{ site.corrections_email }}. Selecting this link opens your email app and pre-fills only this page path in the subject line.
  • On GitHub (public): Open a public issue on GitHub. This requires a free GitHub account, and your GitHub username and message are visible to everyone.

Never include personal health or case details. Whether you send an email or open a GitHub issue, do not include your legal name, Social Security number, Medicaid ID, court or agency case number, immigration status, or private health or household details.
