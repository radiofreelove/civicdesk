Every cited source carries one of four explicit status labels: Official, Not official, Not confirmed, or Our reading. Read what each label means or inspect our registries on GitHub.

Report a mistake

Spot an outdated rule, wrong fee, broken link, or unclear sentence? You can reach us in two ways:

  • By email (no GitHub account needed): Email {{ site.corrections_email }}. Selecting this link opens your email app and pre-fills only this page path in the subject line.
  • On GitHub (public): Open a public issue on GitHub. This requires a free GitHub account, and your GitHub username and message are visible to everyone.

Never include personal health or case details. Whether you send an email or open a GitHub issue, do not include your legal name, Social Security number, Medicaid ID, court or agency case number, immigration status, or private health or household details.
