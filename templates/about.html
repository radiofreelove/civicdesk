  • 2027 Medicaid Work & Activity Rules (/medicaid/): Oregon Health Plan (OHP), Washington Apple Health, and Utah Medicaid.
  • Legal Name, ID & Vital Records (/id-records/): Marriage, divorce, court name changes, and sex or gender designation updates across Oregon, Washington, Utah, Social Security, and passports.

How we verify sources

  1. Primary sources first: Published statutes, administrative rules, court orders, official forms, and agency guidance come before anything else.
  2. Human-reviewed, machine-assisted provenance: Machine tools assist with fetching and comparing official records, and a human maintainer reviews and approves every rule and update before publication.
  3. Four-tier source honesty labels: Every cited source carries one of four explicit status labels: Official, Not official, Not confirmed, or Our reading. Read what each label means.
  4. Public audit trail: Source registries (data/sources/medicaid.yaml and data/sources/id_records.yaml), build checks, and commit history are open for inspection on GitHub.

Report a mistake

Spot an outdated rule, wrong fee, broken link, or unclear sentence? You can reach us in two ways:

  • By email (no GitHub account needed): Email {{ site.corrections_email }}. Selecting this link opens your email app and pre-fills only this page path in the subject line.
  • On GitHub (public): Open a public issue on GitHub. This requires a free GitHub account, and your GitHub username and message are visible to everyone.

Never include personal health or case details. Whether you send an email or open a GitHub issue, do not include your legal name, Social Security number, Medicaid ID, court or agency case number, immigration status, or private health or household details.
