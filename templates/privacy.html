What we collect (and block)

  • Zero tracking or accounts: CivicDesk sets zero cookies, runs zero analytics or advertising scripts, loads zero third-party fonts or CDN assets, and accepts zero form submissions to any server.
  • Interactive tools stay in page memory: Answers in the 2-question router, the 1-minute Medicaid checks, and the ID & records path finder live only in your current browser tab and clear immediately when you reload or close the page.
  • Enforced by Content Security Policy: Every page serves connect-src 'none', form-action 'none', and frame-ancestors 'none' so scripts cannot transmit data off your device.
  • Static edge delivery: CivicDesk is hosted solely on Cloudflare Workers Static Assets with host analytics turned off; Cloudflare processes standard network request data (such as IP addresses) to deliver static files and mitigate network attacks. {{ src('ID-HOST-CLOUDFLARE-PRIVACY') }}

Outbound links to government agencies

Every page sets Referrer-Policy: no-referrer. When you follow a link from CivicDesk to a court or agency website, your browser does not tell that website which CivicDesk page you came from. Once you open an outside website, its own privacy rules apply.

What can be saved on this device

  • Reading settings (text size, color theme, spacing, font, and short version) save in your browser's local storage only when you change them.
  • Step checklists save on your device only if you turn on "Remember my checklist on this device" inside Reading settings.
  • On a shared computer, open Reading settings and select Forget everything saved on this device when you finish.

Scoped "Leave quickly" control

  • Pages in Legal Name, ID & Vital Records and domestic-safety exemption callouts include a calm Leave quickly control (and a triple-Shift keyboard shortcut when not typing in a text box).
  • Using Leave quickly blanks the current screen and replaces the current tab entry with weather.gov.
  • Browser history limit: Replacing the current page does not erase earlier pages you visited in the same tab. For stronger privacy on a monitored device, open a Private or Incognito window before browsing and clear recent history when done.
