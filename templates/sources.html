Four-tier source honesty labels

Official
Confirmed in a published government statute, administrative rule, court order, or agency form or guidance.
Not official
Reported by an external legal aid organization, advocacy group, research survey, or bill tracker rather than published directly by the government agency.
Not confirmed
Mentioned in draft policy or pending guidance, or an agency rule we could not confirm in a published official source. Verify directly with the agency before relying on it.
Our reading
CivicDesk's plain-language synthesis across multiple primary sources where the statute or agency page does not state the point in one sentence. Never legal advice.
