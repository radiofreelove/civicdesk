After court

Update your records in the right order

    {% call m.step(page.slug, 1, 'after-copies', 'Get certified copies of your court order') %}

    Many offices want a certified copy, not a photocopy. Each one has a fee. Ask your court how to order them.

    • Oregon: {{ fact('or_fee_certified_copy') }}. {{ src('ID-OR-OJD-FEES') }}
    • Washington: {{ fact('wa_fee_cert_copy') }} in superior court. {{ src('ID-WA-RCW-36-18-016') }} WashingtonLawHelp suggests getting 7 or more. {{ src('ID-WA-WLH-NAME') }}
    • Utah: {{ fact('ut_fee_cert_copy') }}. {{ src('ID-UT-COURTS-FEES') }}
    {% endcall %} {% call m.step(page.slug, 2, 'after-ssa', 'Social Security (name changes)') %}

    Ask for a replacement card with your new name. You may be able to start online. {{ ext('ID-US-SSA-NAME') }}. {{ src('ID-US-SSA-NAME') }}

    Social Security is not changing sex markers right now. Read why.

    {% endcall %} {% call m.step(page.slug, 3, 'after-license', 'Driver license or state ID') %}
    • Oregon: {{ ext('ID-OR-DMV-GENDER') }}. Choose M, F, or X in person. Bring your court order for a name change. {{ src('ID-OR-DMV-NAME') }}
    • Washington: {{ ext('ID-WA-DOL-GENDER') }}. Choose M, F, or X. In person or by mail.
    • Utah: {{ ext('ID-UT-DLD-NAME') }}. Bring your court order. We could not confirm Utah's process for the sex on a license. {{ m.badge('not_confirmed') }}
    {% endcall %} {% call m.step(page.slug, 4, 'after-birth', 'Birth certificate') %}

    Only the state where you were born can change it.

    • Born in Oregon: a form or your court judgment.
    • Born in Washington: a form for the sex designation; your court order for the name.
    • Born in Utah: your court order and form 902.
    • Born somewhere else: {{ ext('ID-US-CDC-W2W') }}.
    {% endcall %} {% call m.step(page.slug, 5, 'after-passport', 'Passport: read this first') %}

    Any new passport application can come back with the sex assigned at birth, even one for a name change. Read before you apply. {{ src('ID-US-HRC-PASSPORT') }}

{% endcall %} {% call m.callout('good', 'Make it easier on your future self', 'after-tips', extra=true) %}
  • Keep one folder, paper or digital, with your court order, receipts, and dates you sent things.
  • Write down who you talked to and what they said. Offices sometimes give different answers.
  • Do one or two places a day. This is a lot of tasks, and there is no prize for speed.
