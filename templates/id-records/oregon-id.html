How to update your Oregon card

  • Choose M, F, or X. On Oregon cards, X means "not specified." This works for standard and REAL ID cards. {{ src('ID-OR-DMV-GENDER') }}
  • No proof of gender needed. Go in person, by appointment or walk-in, and bring your current card.
  • Cost: {{ fact('or_dmv_fee') }}. Eight-year renewal is $54 for a license or $43 for an ID ($30 extra for REAL ID). {{ src('ID-OR-DMV-GENDER') }} {{ src('ID-OR-DMV-DRIVER-INFO') }}
{% call m.box('note', 'Changing your name too?') %}
  • Update Social Security first. For REAL ID, your card name must match Social Security. For a standard card, DMV can verify under another name with proof of that name. {{ src('ID-OR-DMV-NAME') }}
  • What to bring: an original or certified copy of your court order, plus proof of identity and address. REAL ID needs two address proofs from different sources. {{ src('ID-OR-DMV-NAME') }} {{ src('ID-OR-DMV-PROOF') }} {{ src('ID-OR-DMV-DRIVER-INFO') }}
  • Vehicle titles are separate: changing your license does not update your vehicle title. Update a title by mail or at a DMV office: {{ ext('ID-OR-DMV-NAME') }}. {{ src('ID-OR-DMV-NAME') }}
{% endcall %} {% call m.box('watch', 'Watch out') %}

The DMV keeps a history of every card and name. It shares name, date of birth, and sex with some other systems. These include the Selective Service System and a state police database that federal agencies can use. {{ src('ID-OR-DMV-GENDER') }}
