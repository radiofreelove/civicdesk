Court steps for adults

This is the same process for a name change, a legal sex change, or both. You can do it without a lawyer. {{ src('ID-OR-OJD-ADULT-PACKET') }}

    {% call m.step('oregon', 1, 'or-step-forms', first_step) %}

    Pick one:

    • {{ ext('ID-OR-OJD-GUIDE-FILE') }}: free questions online that fill in the forms for you. Answers must be in English. {{ src('ID-OR-OJD-GUIDE-FILE') }}
    • {{ ext('ID-OR-OJD-ADULT-PACKET') }}: print and fill in by hand. It has a petition and a judgment form.

    All of Oregon's name and sex change forms, including the ones for minors, are on the {{ ext('ID-OR-OJD-FORMS') }}.

    {% endcall %} {% call m.step('oregon', 2, 'or-step-fill', 'Fill out the petition and judgment') %}
    • Check name, legal sex, or both. For sex, choose male, female, or nonbinary (printed as X on Oregon licenses and birth certificates).
    • For a sex change, check the treatment box. It reads: "I have undergone surgical, hormonal, or other treatment appropriate to me for the purpose of affirming my gender identity." The law leaves what counts as appropriate to you. {{ src('ID-OR-ORS-33') }}
    • Answer the "public interest" questions. The form asks about child support you owe, protective or restraining orders against you, probation or parole, and sex offender registration. The judge uses your answers to decide whether the change fits the public interest. A yes does not automatically mean the judge will say no. {{ m.badge('our_reading') }}
    • List names you have used before.
    • Sign it. You sign under penalty of perjury. The form has no notary section.
    {% endcall %} {% call m.step('oregon', 3, 'or-step-file', 'File it at a circuit court') %}
    • Name only: file in the circuit court for the county where you live.
    • Sex, or name and sex together: file in any Oregon circuit court.
    • Pay {{ fact('or_fee_petition') }}, or ask the court to waive or defer the fee. {{ ext('ID-OR-OJD-FEE-WAIVER') }}.

    {{ ext('ID-OR-OJD-COURTS') }}. Write the county name at the top of every form. Some rural counties share a court webpage (Coos & Curry, Grant & Harney, and Umatilla & Morrow).

    {% endcall %} {% call m.step('oregon', 4, 'or-step-wait', 'Wait for the judge') %}

    You usually do not go to court. You only go if the court tells you to or sends you a hearing notice. {{ src('ID-OR-OJD-ADULT-PACKET') }}

    {% endcall %} {% call m.step('oregon', 5, 'or-step-judgment', 'Get your judgment and certified copies') %}

    The change is official once the judge's judgment is entered. The court mails you a Notice of Entry of Judgment. Call the court to get copies of the judgment.

    Offices usually want a certified copy. Each one costs {{ fact('or_fee_certified_copy') }}. {{ src('ID-OR-OJD-FEES') }}

    {% endcall %} {% call m.step('oregon', 6, 'or-step-update', 'Update your records yourself') %}

    The court does not send your judgment to anyone. You take it to each office. Start with Social Security if your name changed, then the DMV. {{ src('ID-OR-OJD-ADULT-PACKET') }}

    Use the after-court checklist.

Who can see your case {{ m.badge('changed') }}

{% call m.callout('changed', 'New in 2026: sex change cases are confidential', 'or-confidential') %}

For cases filed on or after {{ fact('or_confidential_date') }}, a case asking for a legal change of sex is confidential. That includes cases that change name and sex together. {{ src('ID-OR-2026-CH52') }}

  • Who can see it: you and your lawyer, the court, someone a judge allows for good cause, and people who must get notice in a minor's name change.
  • Filed before July 1, 2026? You can ask the court to make your case confidential.
  • Want it public? You can ask the court to make the case, or parts of it, public.
{% endcall %}

Name-only cases are still public. A name change filed with a sex change is confidential. A name change filed alone is not. {{ src('ID-OR-2026-CH52') }}

If you are a certified participant in Oregon's Address Confidentiality Program, you can ask the court to seal a name-only case. The court must seal it unless it finds good cause not to. The form has a box for this. {{ src('ID-OR-ORS-33') }}

Changes for someone under 18

  • Who files: a parent, legal guardian, or legal representative, using the minor forms. {{ ext('ID-OR-OJD-MINOR-PACKET') }}. {{ src('ID-OR-OJD-ADULT-PACKET') }}
  • Where: a minor's name change goes to the county where the child lives. A sex change can go to any circuit court.
  • Notice: for a name change, the court requires written notice to the child's parents and legal guardians. A parent may not need notice if both are true: the child has not lived with that parent, and that parent has not paid or tried to pay support. {{ src('ID-OR-ORS-33') }}
  • Birth certificate: a parent or legal guardian can use the OHA 2673 form for a child born in Oregon. See Oregon birth certificate steps. {{ src('ID-OR-OHA-GENDER') }}

Under 18 and your parents will not help? Talk with a lawyer. Oregon law lets a judge meet with a minor who applies for a name change, without parents in the room. A lawyer can tell you whether that path fits you. {{ src('ID-OR-ORS-33') }} {{ m.badge('our_reading') }}

Good questions

{% call m.qa("Do I need a doctor's letter or proof of surgery?", 'q-doctor') %}

The court form does not ask for medical records. You check a box swearing that you have had treatment that is appropriate for you. The law says "surgical, hormonal or other treatment," so surgery is not required. {{ src('ID-OR-ORS-33') }} {{ src('ID-OR-OJD-ADULT-PACKET') }}

{% endcall %} {% call m.qa("I don't live in Oregon. Can I file here?", 'q-out-of-state') %}

For a name change, the law says to file in the county where you live, so you need to live in Oregon. {{ src('ID-OR-ORS-33') }}

For a legal sex change, the law says "any circuit court in this state" may hear it. The part we read does not mention where you live. We have not found an official answer for people who live in another state. Talk with a lawyer before you rely on this. {{ m.badge('our_reading') }}

{% endcall %} {% call m.qa("What will the judge decide?", 'q-judge') %}

Oregon law says the court "shall" grant a name change unless it finds the change "is not consistent with the public interest." A sex change follows the same rule. {{ m.badge('our_reading') }} {{ src('ID-OR-ORS-33') }}

{% endcall %} {% call m.qa("I filed before July 1, 2026. Is my old case public?", 'q-old-case') %}

It may be. The 2026 law applies to cases filed on or after July 1, 2026. For earlier sex change cases, you can file a motion asking the court to make the case confidential. {{ src('ID-OR-2026-CH52') }}

{% endcall %} {% call m.qa("Will an Oregon judgment change my birth certificate from another state?", 'q-other-birth-state') %}

Not by itself. Each state's vital records office has its own rules. Some accept an out-of-state court order and some do not. Find your birth state's office with {{ ext('ID-US-CDC-W2W') }}.

{% endcall %}
