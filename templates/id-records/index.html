Common ways a legal name or record updates

  • Marriage or registered domestic partnership: Your certified marriage or partnership certificate is your legal name-change proof for Social Security, your state driver license or ID, and your passport—no separate court petition needed. {{ src('ID-US-SSA-NAME') }} {{ src('ID-OR-ORS-106') }}
  • Divorce or dissolution: Ask to restore a former name in your divorce or dissolution judgment, then use a certified copy of that signed decree at Social Security and the DMV. {{ src('ID-OR-ORS-107') }} {{ src('ID-WA-RCW-26-09-150') }}
  • Court-ordered name change: File an adult or minor court petition in your state, including sealed or confidential options where state law provides them. {{ src('ID-OR-ORS-33') }} {{ src('ID-WA-RCW-4-24-130') }} {{ src('ID-UT-CODE-42-1') }}
  • Sex or gender designation updates: Oregon and Washington update state licenses and birth certificates with an agency form (M, F, or X, no court needed), while Utah requires a district court order and federal offices follow separate 2025–2026 rules. {{ src('ID-OR-DMV-GENDER') }} {{ src('ID-WA-DOL-GENDER') }} {{ src('ID-UT-CODE-26B-8-111') }}
