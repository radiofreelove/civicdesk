On November 4, 2026, Washington DOH retired the old Affidavit for Correction (DOH 422-034) and replaced it with separate amendment forms on {{ ext('ID-WA-DOH-AMEND-BIRTH') }}. {{ src('ID-WA-DOH-AMEND-BIRTH') }}

For a sex-designation change (DOH 422-143 or DOH 422-144) or a court-ordered name change (DOH 422-126), keep using those dedicated forms. How adults update a Washington birth certificate

This is for people born in Washington. {{ src('ID-WA-DOH-SEX') }}

  • Choose M, F, or X. X means a gender that is not exclusively male or female.
  • Adults and emancipated minors use {{ ext('ID-WA-DOH-422-143') }}. Sign it in front of a notary.
  • A court order or a doctor's letter is not accepted for the sex designation. Use the form.
  • If your legal name is different from your birth certificate, add a certified court order and {{ ext('ID-WA-DOH-422-126') }}.
  • No fee to make the change. New certified copies are {{ fact('wa_doh_cert_fee') }}.
  • Send by mail or bring it in person by appointment. Plan for 4 to 6 weeks under Washington DOH's November 2026 birth-amendment notice above (the sex-designation page previously listed about 3 business days, plus up to 2 weeks for mail).

Under 18

For a minor born in Washington, use {{ ext('ID-WA-DOH-422-144') }} to change the sex designation. A parent or guardian and a licensed provider sign it. {{ src('ID-WA-DOH-SEX') }}

Washington DOH's sex-designation page still tells parents to use the old Affidavit for Correction (DOH 422-034) to change a minor's first or middle names without a court order. DOH retired form 422-034 on November 4, 2026. To change a minor's first or middle names without a court order, use the new Application to Amend a Birth Certificate on {{ ext('ID-WA-DOH-AMEND-BIRTH') }} instead. {{ src('ID-WA-DOH-AMEND-BIRTH') }} {{ src('ID-WA-DOH-SEX') }}

{% call m.qa("Does Washington change the gender marker on a birth certificate from another state?", 'q-other-state') %}

No. Only the state where you were born can change your birth certificate. A Washington court order confirming your gender may help if that state asks for one. Learn about the optional court order. Find your birth state's office with {{ ext('ID-US-CDC-W2W') }}.
