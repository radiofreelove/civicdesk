How to complete your DOL update

  • Gender designation only: Update a standard card at a DOL office or by mailing form 520043 (watch for DOL's follow-up letter); Enhanced EDL/EID cards require an in-person appointment plus form 520043 (no online marker option). {{ src('ID-WA-DOL-GENDER') }}
  • Adding a legal name change: Update Social Security first, book a DOL appointment, and bring identity proof plus a certified marriage certificate (not ceremonial), divorce decree, or court order, along with the standard replacement card fee. {{ src('ID-WA-DOL-NAME') }}
  • Sealed Superior Court orders & vehicles: DOL's Identity Management Team assists with sealed orders at dol-psdidentitymanagement@dol.wa.gov (see {{ ext('ID-WA-WLH-NAME') }}). Update vehicle titles and registrations separately. {{ src('ID-WA-DOL-NAME') }} {{ src('ID-WA-WLH-NAME') }}
