How to update your Washington card

  • Choose M, F, or X. {{ src('ID-WA-DOL-GENDER') }}
  • In person: make an appointment, bring your current card, and say which marker you want.
  • Enhanced license or ID (EDL or EID), in person: make an appointment. Bring your current card and the filled-out Change of Gender Designation Request form (520043).
  • By mail: send the Change of Gender Designation Request form (520043). The department then mails you a letter about getting your new card.
  • There is no online option for the gender change itself.
  • Cost: {{ fact('wa_dol_fee') }}. {{ src('ID-WA-DOL-GENDER') }}
