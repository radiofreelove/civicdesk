How to update a Utah birth certificate

  • For people born in Utah. Utah's law applies only to birth certificates the state issued. {{ src('ID-UT-CODE-26B-8-111') }}
  • Send three things to Utah Vital Records: a certified copy of the signed order, {{ ext('ID-UT-VITAL-902-2026') }}, and the fee. {{ src('ID-UT-COURTS-SEX-CHANGE') }}
  • Cost: {{ fact('ut_vital_amend_fee') }}. {{ src('ID-UT-VITAL-FEES') }} {{ src('ID-UT-COURTS-SEX-CHANGE') }}
  • It can take several weeks. Mailing address: {{ m.copyable(fact('ut_vital_mail'), 'mailing address') }}
  • After the court order, Vital Records issues a new birth certificate that does not show which parts were changed, unless you ask it to.
  • Vital Records also issues an amendment history that lists the changed fields and the date, and keeps a record of every amendment.
