Accessibility

Accessibility and inclusive communication

{{ m.lede("CivicDesk follows UK Home Office and GOV.UK inclusive communication standards, W3C cognitive accessibility guidance, and WCAG 2.2 AAA reading contrast.") }}

Built-in guardrails

  • High reading contrast: Every reading text color pair in the Civic Horizon palette meets at least a 7:1 contrast ratio (WCAG 2.2 AAA), and interactive borders and marks meet at least 3:1. {{ src('ID-STD-WCAG22') }}
  • Left-aligned sentence case: All headings, labels, and lists are left-aligned in sentence case, with no italics and short, literal sentences. {{ src('ID-STD-COGA') }}
  • 200% and 400% zoom reflow: Multi-step diagrams and tables use semantic HTML lists and stack into a single column at narrow widths so you never have to scroll sideways at 200% or 400% browser zoom.
  • Generous touch targets: Buttons, navigation links, radio buttons, checkboxes, and disclosure summaries provide at least 44 by 44 pixel tap areas where possible (and strictly at least 24 by 24 pixels everywhere).
  • Works without JavaScript: Every guide page can be read from top to bottom with JavaScript turned off.

Reading settings on every page

  • Text size: Standard, Large, or Extra large.
  • Color themes: Match device, Plain white paper, Warm paper, Dark slate, or High contrast.
  • Wide spacing: Extra letter, word, and line spacing meeting WCAG 1.4.12.
  • Bundled Atkinson Hyperlegible Next font: Designed by the Braille Institute so similar letters and numbers are easier to distinguish, served locally with zero external font requests.
  • Short version toggle: Hides optional background sections on long guides while keeping every core step, fee, deadline, and print view complete.

Tell us if something gets in your way

If any page is hard to read with a screen reader, keyboard, magnifier, or mobile device, please report an accessibility issue so we can fix it.
