Reading settings on every page

  • Text size: Standard, Large, or Extra large.
  • Color themes: Match device, Plain white paper, Warm paper, Dark slate, or High contrast.
  • Wide spacing: Extra letter, word, and line spacing meeting WCAG 1.4.12.
  • Bundled Atkinson Hyperlegible Next font: Designed by the Braille Institute so similar letters and numbers are easier to distinguish, served locally with zero external font requests.
  • Short version toggle: Hides optional background sections on long guides while keeping every core step, fee, deadline, and print view complete.
