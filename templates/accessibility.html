Accessibility and inclusive communication

{{ m.lede("CivicDesk follows UK Home Office and GOV.UK inclusive communication standards, W3C cognitive accessibility guidance, and WCAG 2.2 AAA reading contrast.") }} {{ m.onthispage([ ('standards', 'Built-in guardrails'), ('controls', 'Reading settings'), ('feedback', 'Accessibility feedback') ]) }}

Built-in guardrails

  • 7:1+ WCAG 2.2 AAA contrast: Every reading text pair across all four color themes meets at least a 7:1 contrast ratio, and interactive borders and graphical marks meet at least 3:1. {{ src('ID-STD-WCAG22') }}
  • Plain-language cognitive clarity: Left-aligned sentence case headings, concrete everyday words, upright emphasis without italics, and short literal sentences (`<= 65ch` measure). {{ src('ID-STD-COGA') }}
  • 200% & 400% zoom reflow: Multi-column diagrams and tables collapse cleanly into a single vertical column at narrow widths (`320px`) with zero horizontal scrolling.
  • 44x44 px touch targets & no-JavaScript support: Standalone interactive controls provide at least `44x44 px` tap areas, high-visibility focus rings, and full top-to-bottom readability when JavaScript is disabled.

Reading settings on every page

  • Text size: Standard, Large, or Extra large (`150%`).
  • Color themes: Match device, Plain white paper, Warm paper, Dark slate, or High contrast.
  • Wide spacing: Extra letter, word, and line spacing meeting WCAG 1.4.12.
  • Bundled Atkinson Hyperlegible Next font: Designed by the Braille Institute so similar letters and numerals are easier to distinguish, served locally with zero external font requests.
  • Short version toggle: Folds away optional background sections on long guides while keeping every core step, fee, deadline, and print view complete.
