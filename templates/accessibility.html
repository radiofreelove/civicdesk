Built-in guardrails

  • 7:1+ WCAG 2.2 AAA contrast: Every reading text pair across all four color palettes (plus automatic Match my device mode) meets at least a 7:1 contrast ratio, and interactive borders and graphical marks meet at least 3:1. {{ src('ID-STD-WCAG22') }}
  • Plain-language cognitive clarity: Left-aligned sentence case headings, concrete everyday words, upright emphasis without italics, and short literal sentences, with lines kept to about 65 characters. {{ src('ID-STD-COGA') }}
  • 200% & 400% zoom reflow: Multi-column diagrams and tables collapse cleanly into a single vertical column on screens as narrow as 320 pixels, with no sideways scrolling.
  • 44x44 px touch targets & no-JavaScript support: Standalone interactive controls provide tap areas of at least 44 by 44 pixels, high-visibility focus rings, and full top-to-bottom readability when JavaScript is disabled.

Reading settings on every page

  • Text size: Standard, Large, or Extra large (150%).
  • Color themes: Match my device (automatic), Newsprint (standard), Plain white paper, Dark ink, or High contrast.
  • Wide spacing: Extra letter, word, and line spacing meeting WCAG 1.4.12.
    • {% if fonts_available %}
  • Self-hosted fonts, including Atkinson Hyperlegible Next: Pages default to self-hosted Source Serif 4 and let you switch to Atkinson Hyperlegible Next (designed by the Braille Institute so similar letters and numerals are easier to distinguish), with zero external font requests.
    • {% else %}
  • Self-hosted reading font: Pages use self-hosted Source Serif 4 with zero external font requests.
    • {% endif %}
  • Short version toggle: Folds away optional background sections on long guides while keeping every core step, fee, deadline, and print view complete.
  • Quick-exit shortcut option: Turn off the triple-Shift keyboard shortcut if it conflicts with Sticky Keys or a screen reader; the Leave quickly button stays active.
